Meet Kabous
The Mechanic Behind VDW

Kabous van der Westhuizen

With over 15 years of hands-on experience in the automotive industry, Kabous has built VDW Development & Services into one of the most trusted workshops in the Swartland region.

Kabous specialises in Toyota, Nissan, Volkswagen, and Opel vehicles. As an authorised Powerflow Exhaust installer, he offers custom stainless steel exhaust systems backed by Powerflow's lifetime guarantee.

Whether you speak English or Afrikaans, Kabous takes the time to explain every repair clearly and honestly. No hidden costs, no unnecessary work.

What We Do
Full-Spectrum Auto Care

From routine servicing to complex engine rebuilds – everything handled under one roof.

01

Full Servicing & Maintenance

Regular oil and filter changes, spark plugs, air filters, fluid top-ups and full multi-point inspections. We follow manufacturer service schedules to maintain your warranty.

02

Engine Repairs & Rebuilds

Complete engine overhauls, head gasket replacements, timing belt and chain work, cylinder head skimming, and piston ring replacement on petrol and diesel engines.

03

OBD Computer Diagnostics

Advanced OBD-II diagnostic scanning to read fault codes, live sensor data, and system parameters. We pinpoint electrical faults and engine management issues quickly.

04

Brakes, Suspension & Steering

Brake pad and disc replacement, shock absorbers, control arms, tie-rod ends, wheel bearings, wheel alignment and balancing.

05

Powerflow Exhaust Systems

Custom stainless steel exhaust systems designed and fitted in-house. Every system comes with Powerflow's lifetime guarantee.

06

Pre-Purchase Inspections & Sales

Thorough 60-point mechanical inspection before you buy. We also sell quality pre-owned vehicles with service history and roadworthy certificates.
